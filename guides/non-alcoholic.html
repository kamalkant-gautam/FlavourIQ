Non-Alcoholic Beverages

Every Sip, Zero Proof

From the first cup of tea to a crafted mocktail: how alcohol-free drinks are grouped, made and served. Clear enough for a first day, deep enough for a seasoned professional.

What counts as non-alcoholic?

A non-alcoholic beverage contains no alcohol, or only a trace. Many countries set the limit at under 0.5% ABV; labels such as “alcohol-free” and “0.0%” are stricter in some places, so always follow local label law. These drinks are not a side note: they serve everyone who does not drink alcohol, and they carry some of the best margins on a menu.

Four families

Stimulating, Refreshing, Nourishing, and Modern Zero-Proof & Functional. Each section opens with an animated glass that shows how a typical drink from that family is built.

Hot or cold?

Stimulating drinks are traditionally hot, refreshing drinks cold, nourishing drinks either, and zero-proof drinks are almost always built cold.

Zero-proof sour (2 : 1 : 1)

2 oz base (chilled tea, cold brew or non-alcoholic spirit) + 1 oz fresh citrus + 1 oz sweet. Shake and fine-strain.

Highball / spritz balance

1.5 oz fruit purée or shrub + 0.75 oz fresh acid + 4 oz soda or tonic, built over fresh cubes.
